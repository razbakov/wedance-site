Growth & Story

Seeking peopleActive — supports the foundation experiment
Take a first issue → Say hello

Driver

The foundation experiment only means something if real dancers and organizers show up to it. This circle brings the audience — and tells WeDance's story clearly enough that people want in.

Accountabilities

  • Grow reach to dancers and organizers across the cities we're live in.
  • Turn what the product does into a story people share.
  • Keep the brand consistent across the app, the org doc, and social.

Roles open

  • Content / Marketing lead — own the cadence: what we publish, where, and how it converts.
  • Designer — visual identity, event posters, landing pages, in-app polish.

Start here

  • Take a `good first issue` tagged content or design, and open a PR.
  • Or say hello with one thing you'd change first.

Good fit

You've grown a community or a brand from near-zero, or you design fast and care about how a product feels — and you'd rather ship a real thing this week than plan a campaign for next quarter.

WeDance is a dance-community platform by Alex Razbakov, run in the open with Sociocracy 3.0.

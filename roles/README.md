# Open circles — how to join WeDance

WeDance runs in the open with Sociocracy 3.0. We don't post job ads — we open **circles**.
Each circle owns a domain, is driven by a real need, and is scaffolded day-to-day by AI agents.
An **open role** means a human can take one over or work alongside the agent.

This directory is the **single source of truth** for what's open. The "Open circles" section on
[org.wedance.vip](https://org.wedance.vip/#circles) renders from these files — edit here, not the HTML.

## How joining works

1. **Read** the circle you're drawn to (files below).
2. **Take a first issue** — grab a `good first issue` and open a PR. Contribution is the interview.
3. **Consent** — a short call; the circle consents you into the role (an S3 role election).
4. **Land inside** — you get the team room, the Telegram working group, and your circle's weekly rhythm.

No CV, no cover letter. What you ship is the application.

## Role schema

Every circle/role file carries these fields:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Circle** | The domain it belongs to |
| **Status** | `seeking-human` · `agent-run` · `dormant` |
| **Driver** | The need that makes this role exist |
| **Accountabilities** | What the role is on the hook for |
| **Roles open** | The specific seats a human can take |
| **Start here** | The first issue / first contribution |
| **Good fit** | Who thrives here |

`Status` values:
- **seeking-human** — actively looking for a person to own or co-own this.
- **agent-run** — an AI agent runs it today; a human is welcome to take it over.
- **dormant** — activates once the foundation experiment is proven; not staffing yet.

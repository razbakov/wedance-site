# Festival Experience

- **Circle:** Festival Experience
- **Status:** seeking-human
- **Phase:** Active — the foundation experiment

## Driver

The whole platform rests on one assumption: dancers will use a digital interactive schedule
instead of static images. If that's false, nothing else matters — so we test it at one real
festival before building anything on top. This circle owns that test.

## Accountabilities

- Get a real festival's schedule into the app and in front of real dancers.
- Measure whether dancers actually use it, then take the data back to the organizer.
- Keep the experiment honest — real data, no vanity metrics.

## Roles open

- **Organizer-partnerships lead** — land the festival that runs the experiment; be the human the organizer talks to.
- **Frontend engineer** — build and polish the interactive schedule (Nuxt / Vue).

## Start here

- Grab a [`good first issue`](https://github.com/razbakov/wedance-2026/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22) in the app repo and open a PR.
- Or [say hello](mailto:hello@wedance.vip?subject=Festival%20Experience%20circle) with the festival you'd bring.

## Good fit

You've run or been close to a dance festival, or you like shipping small, measurable product
experiments and talking to real users rather than guessing.

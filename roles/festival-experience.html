Driver

The whole platform rests on one assumption: dancers will use a digital interactive schedule instead of static images. If that's false, nothing else matters — so we test it at one real festival before building anything on top. This circle owns that test.

Accountabilities

  • Get a real festival's schedule into the app and in front of real dancers.
  • Measure whether dancers actually use it, then take the data back to the organizer.
  • Keep the experiment honest — real data, no vanity metrics.

Roles open

  • Organizer-partnerships lead — land the festival that runs the experiment; be the human the organizer talks to.
  • Frontend engineer — build and polish the interactive schedule (Nuxt / Vue).

Start here

  • Grab a `good first issue` in the app repo and open a PR.
  • Or say hello with the festival you'd bring.

Good fit

You've run or been close to a dance festival, or you like shipping small, measurable product experiments and talking to real users rather than guessing.

WeDance is a dance-community platform by Alex Razbakov, run in the open with Sociocracy 3.0.

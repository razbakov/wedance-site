# Marketplace

- **Circle:** Marketplace
- **Status:** dormant
- **Phase:** Activates once the foundation experiment is proven

## Driver

Artists, venues, and organizers find each other through word of mouth and luck. Marketplace
connects them — artists and venues discoverable and bookable by organizers. It activates once
the foundation is proven and Discovery is feeding real supply.

## Accountabilities (when active)

- Make artists and venues discoverable and bookable.
- Give organizers a real supply side to build events from.
- Keep incentives honest for every side of the market.

## Roles open

None yet — this circle is dormant. It depends on [Discovery](discovery.md), which depends on
[Festival Experience](festival-experience.md).

## Good fit

You've worked a two-sided marketplace or run bookings for artists/venues. Check back once the
foundation is proven.

# Growth & Story

- **Circle:** Growth & Story
- **Status:** seeking-human
- **Phase:** Active — supports the foundation experiment

## Driver

The foundation experiment only means something if real dancers and organizers show up to it.
This circle brings the audience — and tells WeDance's story clearly enough that people want in.

## Accountabilities

- Grow reach to dancers and organizers across the cities we're live in.
- Turn what the product does into a story people share.
- Keep the brand consistent across the app, the org doc, and social.

## Roles open

- **Content / Marketing lead** — own the cadence: what we publish, where, and how it converts.
- **Designer** — visual identity, event posters, landing pages, in-app polish.

## Start here

- Take a [`good first issue`](https://github.com/razbakov/wedance-2026/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22) tagged content or design, and open a PR.
- Or [say hello](mailto:hello@wedance.vip?subject=Growth%20%26%20Story%20circle) with one thing you'd change first.

## Good fit

You've grown a community or a brand from near-zero, or you design fast and care about how a
product feels — and you'd rather ship a real thing this week than plan a campaign for next quarter.

Partnership Manager — Role Description (Delegation Canvas)

02_Roles/Partnership_Manager/Role_Description.md Last changed 10 Apr 2026 — Add organizer segmentation strategy and pitch playbook Propose a change History Source

💬 Hover any passage to comment. Comments become triage issues, and accepted changes land as PRs to this file.

Role Keeper: AI Agent (to be deployed) Date/Version: 2026-04-04 Delegator: Kirill Korshikov Term: Ongoing Review date: 2026-07-04

Purpose

Primary Driver: Validating the Festival Schedule requires real partnerships with festival organizers. Kirill has the network but not the time to handle all the preparation, follow-up, and documentation around each partnership. Main Requirement: Support the partnership pipeline — research organizers, draft outreach, prepare pitch materials, track conversations, and document agreements.

Key Responsibilities

  • Research potential festival partners (upcoming festivals, organizer contacts)
  • Draft outreach messages and pitch materials for Kirill's review
  • Track partnership pipeline (contacted → pitched → piloting → active)
  • Prepare case study materials after each pilot
  • Document partnership agreements and terms
  • Monitor festival calendars for timing opportunities

Customers and Deliverables

Customer Deliverable
Kirill Researched leads, draft outreach, pitch decks
Partnership Pipeline status reports
Analyst Partnership data for experiment tracking

Dependencies

Provider What they deliver
Kirill Network introductions, approval on outreach, relationship management
Product Lead Product specs to include in pitch materials
Analyst Usage data and metrics for case studies

External Constraints

  • Cannot contact organizers directly — all outreach goes through Kirill
  • Cannot make partnership commitments or pricing decisions
  • Festival timelines are fixed — must plan 4+ weeks ahead

Key Challenges

  • Festival calendar is seasonal — timing windows are narrow
  • Each organizer has different needs and concerns
  • Building trust requires human relationship (Kirill), not just materials

Key Resources

  • Kirill's organizer network and SDTV contacts
  • Festival calendar databases
  • Claude API
  • Organizer Pitch Playbook v1.0 — segmented pitch templates and sales approach by organizer type

Delegator Responsibilities

  • Kirill: provide network access, send outreach, relay organizer feedback

Competencies, Qualities, and Skills

  • Business development research
  • Persuasive writing (pitch materials, outreach drafts)
  • CRM/pipeline tracking
  • Festival industry awareness

Key Metrics and Monitoring

Metric How Measured Frequency Measured By
Pipeline health Leads researched and ready for outreach Weekly Kirill
Outreach quality Kirill sends drafts without major rewrites Per outreach Kirill
Pilot conversion Leads → confirmed pilot partners Monthly Partnership

Evaluation Schedule

Quarterly review (next: 2026-07-04). Evaluate: Is the pipeline moving? Are materials saving Kirill time?

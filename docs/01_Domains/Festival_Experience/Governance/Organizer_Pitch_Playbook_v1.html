Organizer Pitch Playbook v1.0

Author: Kirill Korshikov Date: 2026-04-10 Status: Active Review date: 2026-07-04 Used by: Partnership Manager, Marketing Lead, Kirill

Core Philosophy

We do not sell a product, a platform, or a new category. We sell three concrete outcomes:

  1. Less confusion before the festival
  2. More engagement and buzz before day one
  3. No new workload for the organizer

Anchor phrase:

We do not create another channel. We turn the information you already have into a better pre-event experience for dancers.

Why This Matters

When a product is new, organizers cannot classify it correctly. They default to familiar boxes: "another website," "another channel," "another thing to manage." At that point we lose, even if the idea is strong.

Therefore: never sell the concept. Sell an obvious improvement to a process they already know.

Not "we built a product." Not "interactive festival page." Not "pre-event activation layer."

Instead: "We take what you already have and in 1-2 days turn it into a clearer experience for dancers before arrival, with no new workload for your team."

Outcome-Led, Not Feature-Led

Never lead with:

  • Interactive schedule
  • Festival page
  • Digital tool

Always lead with:

  • Clearer experience before arrival
  • More buzz before day one
  • Less confusion for dancers
  • Less repeated explanation for the organizer
  • More value from the festival information they already have

We sell compression of complexity, not innovation.

Organizer Segmentation

Type 1 — "We build our own digital ecosystem"

Profile: Already has website, digital tools, maybe AI experiments. Strong digital ownership. Fear: "Another channel," "another platform," "another thing to manage."

Selling mechanics:

  • Do not threaten their ecosystem — reinforce it
  • Sell a lighter layer, not a new product
  • Less extra work, better use of existing materials
  • More engagement without extra management

Language of effect for Type 1:

  • Your dancers understand the event faster
  • Your festival feels more alive before arrival
  • People ask fewer repetitive questions
  • You do not need to build or manage another tool
  • It works from materials you already have

Type 1, Version 1 — First short contact

This is not meant to replace your website or create another channel for your team.

The idea is much simpler: we use the festival information you already have and turn it into a clearer pre-event experience for dancers, so they understand the event faster, feel more engaged before arrival, and ask fewer repetitive questions.

So the value is not "one more platform," but a lighter layer that helps your current ecosystem work better.

Type 1, Version 2 — Stronger, when there is interest

What we do is not a replacement for your website or your digital tools.

We take the information you already have, like schedule, classes, artists, venue details, and side activities, and turn it into a lightweight pre-event experience that makes the festival easier to understand and more exciting before arrival.

The benefit is simple: more clarity for dancers, more buzz before day one, and less confusion without creating a new system for your team to manage.

Type 2 — "Chaos, scattered info, everything manual"

Profile: No strong digital presence. Info spread across stories, chats, images, links. Lots of manual work. Pain: Tons of questions, chaos, people don't understand the program.

Selling mechanics:

  • Do not lead with technology — lead with chaos reduction
  • Fewer questions, less confusion
  • Easier planning, smoother attendee experience
  • Clearer event before arrival

Type 2, Version 1 — First short contact

We can help make the festival much easier for dancers before they arrive.

Instead of collecting information from many places, dancers get one clear experience where they can understand the schedule, workshops, artists, and useful festival details faster and prepare better in advance.

That means less confusion, fewer repeated questions, and a smoother experience before the event even starts.

Type 2, Version 2 — Stronger, when there is interest

A lot of festival confusion happens before the event, not during it.

People look through stories, chats, images, and different links, but still do not feel fully clear about the schedule, classes, activities, or how to prepare. What we do is take the information you already have and turn it into one simple pre-event experience that helps dancers understand the festival faster, plan better, and feel more ready before arrival.

So the value is very practical: less chaos, more clarity, and a festival that already feels alive before day one.

First Contact Structure

Every first touch follows three steps:

  1. One clear observation (familiar problem)
  2. One concrete outcome (what changes)
  3. Low-friction ask (2 minutes, no commitment)

Template:

A lot of festival confusion happens before the event, not during it. I made a simple example of how your existing festival info could feel much clearer for dancers before arrival, without adding another channel for your team to manage. If you want, I can show it in 2 minutes.

Tailored Sample Approach

Until we have real case studies, use tailored samples instead of generic demos.

Not: "Look at our product." Instead: "We took your festival and showed how it could look for your dancers."

This is a critical difference. A big organizer does not want to understand a new category. They want to see in 20 seconds:

  • What changes
  • What's the benefit
  • How much effort it requires
  • Why it does not conflict with their current system

Approach:

"I put together an example based on your festival. The point is not a new platform — it's about making it easier for your dancers to understand the festival before arrival, without you having to manage anything extra. If you're interested, I can show it in 2 minutes."

Two-Layer Pitch System

Layer 1 — "2-minute understanding"

Short text or voice message using the templates above. Goal: get a "show me" response.

Layer 2 — "Tailored visual example for your festival"

A concrete mockup/demo built from their actual festival data. Goal: make the value undeniable without forcing them to imagine a new category.

Without the visual, they will still hear "another digital thing."

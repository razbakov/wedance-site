Content Plan

01_Domains/Festival_Experience/Marketing/meetup-planner/content-plan.md Last changed 7 Apr 2026 — Initial commit: WeDance S3 governance structure Propose a change History Source

💬 Hover any passage to comment. Comments become triage issues, and accepted changes land as PRs to this file.

Campaign: "More Than Just Dancing" Duration: 4 weeks per festival cycle (2 weeks pre-launch → 1 week activation → festival week → 1 week post) Channels: Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp groups, Facebook groups, Email

Week 1 — Awareness (Day 1–7)

Goal: Introduce the problem. No selling yet.

Day Channel Format Content
1 Instagram, TT Reel (15-30s) "Going to [Festival] alone?" — POV: you booked tickets, hotel… but you're eating alone, commuting alone. Text overlay + trending audio
2 Instagram Story (poll) "At your last festival, did you eat dinner alone at least once?" → Yes / More than once
3 Instagram, TT Reel (15s) "The festival ends at midnight. Then what?" — Quick cuts: empty hallway, solo taxi, scrolling phone in hotel. End card: "There's a fix"
4 Instagram Carousel (5 sl) "5 things nobody tells you about going to a dance festival" — 1. You'll eat alone 2. Taxis are expensive solo 3. Roommate roulette 4. You'll forget the workshops 5. The best moments are unplanned
5 TikTok Reel (15s) "Still looking for a roommate?" — Skit: scrolling Facebook groups desperately. End: "There's a better way"
6 Instagram Story (quiz) "How many festivals have you been to this year?" — 1 / 2-3 / 4+ / I lost count
7 Instagram Single image Quote card: "You'll take 200 workshops. You'll remember 1 dinner." — Sunset Vibes style, warm gradients

Week 2 — Solution & Urgency (Day 8–14)

Goal: Reveal WeDance. Open free spots. Create FOMO.

Day Channel Format Content
8 Instagram, TT Reel (30s) Product reveal: "What if someone matched you with 4 dancers for dinner, found you a ride, and a roommate?" — Screen recording of app + real reactions
8 Instagram Story "10 FREE spots just opened for [Festival] → link in bio"
9 WhatsApp groups Text message Ambassador posts template message in 5 festival-specific groups (see campaign.md)
9 Instagram Story Countdown: "9 free spots left" — branded graphic template
10 Instagram, TT Reel (20s) "Same festival. Better crew." — Split screen: solo experience vs. WeDance group experience
10 Facebook groups Long post Festival-specific post with context: what WeDance is, what activities are available, free spots remaining
11 Instagram Story Countdown: "7 free spots left" + welcome new sign-up: "Welcome [Name] from [City]!"
12 Instagram Carousel (4 sl) "How it works" — Step 1: Pick your festival. Step 2: Choose activities (rides, rooms, dinners). Step 3: Get matched. Step 4: Meet your crew
12 Instagram Story Countdown: "5 free spots left"
13 TikTok Reel (15s) "Your next dance partner might also need a ride" — Hook that bridges dance passion to practical logistics
13 Instagram Story Countdown: "3 free spots left" — urgency peak
14 Instagram Story "LAST FREE SPOT" or "Free spots are gone! €1 to join →"
14 Email Newsletter "We're going to [Festival] — here's what's happening" — recap of activities, social proof from sign-ups, CTA

Week 3 — Festival Week (Day 15–21)

Goal: Real-time content. Convert free → paid. Build social proof library.

Day Channel Format Content
Fri AM Instagram Story "Tonight's dinner group has been matched! Check your DMs"
Fri PM Instagram Story "Your restaurant will be revealed in 2 hours…"
Fri Eve Instagram Story Restaurant reveal + map link + "Let's go!"
Fri Night Instagram Story Candid photo from group dinner — "This just happened"
Sat AM Instagram, TT Reel (15-30s) Participant testimonial: "I met these amazing people last night" — raw, authentic, phone-quality
Sat Instagram Story Carpool crew photo: "These 4 just shared a ride from [City]"
Sat Instagram Story Paid conversion push: "12 dancers already in. Are you?" — show names + cities
Sun AM Instagram Story "Breakfast with my new festival crew" — organic participant content (reshare)
Sun Instagram Carousel Live recap: "Day 2 at [Festival] with WeDance" — dinner photos, ride photos, group selfies
Sun Eve Instagram Story "Day 2 dinner group matched! Different people, same vibes"
Mon Instagram, TT Reel (30s) Festival wrap: quick montage of all WeDance moments — rides, dinners, meetups. End card: "Next: [Festival 2]"

Participant-generated content to collect:

  • Carpool selfies (ask before departure)
  • Group dinner candids (capture during + after)
  • "How was it?" video testimonials (15-30s, ask Sunday)
  • Morning-after "breakfast crew" stories (reshare from participants)

Week 4 — Post-Festival & Bridge (Day 22–28)

Goal: Harvest content. Retarget. Seed next festival.

Day Channel Format Content
22 Email Survey Google Form: NPS, testimonial, photo permission, "Would you use this at your next festival?"
23 Instagram Carousel (5 sl) Recap carousel: "What happened when [X] dancers did more than just dance at [Festival]" — Hook → Stats → Quote → Photo → CTA
24 Instagram Story Best testimonial quote card — Sunset Vibes style
25 TikTok Reel (30s) Before/after: "What I expected at [Festival] vs. what actually happened" — solo clips vs. group fun
26 Instagram Single image Announcement: "Next up: [Festival 2]. First 10 free →" — restart the cycle
27 Instagram Story Behind the scenes: "How we match dinner groups" — builds trust + curiosity for next festival
28 Email Newsletter "[Festival] recap + what's next" — stats, best photos, testimonials, next festival announcement with early access CTA

Evergreen Content (post as needed, reuse across festivals)

Topic Format Content
Pain point Reel "Festival bathroom confessions" — things dancers think but don't say
Education Carousel "What is WeDance?" — explainer for cold audiences
Social proof Reel Compilation of testimonial clips from multiple festivals
Community Story highlight "Our dancers" — reshared participant content, organized by festival
Behind the scenes Reel "How the matching algorithm works" — simplified, visual
Founder story Carousel/Reel "Why I built WeDance" — personal story, dance community connection

Content Production Checklist

Before each festival cycle

  • Customize all posts with festival name, dates, city
  • Update countdown graphics template with festival branding
  • Prepare WhatsApp/Facebook group message (adapt template from campaign.md)
  • Brief ambassadors (3-5) on posting schedule and talking points
  • Set up UTM links per channel: ?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=reel&utm_campaign=[festival]
  • Schedule Week 1-2 posts in advance (Later, Buffer, or native scheduling)
  • Print posters and tent cards with dynamic QR codes

During festival

  • Capture minimum 10 photos and 3 video testimonials
  • Post real-time stories as moments happen
  • Reshare all participant-tagged content within 1 hour
  • Track sign-up numbers for countdown updates

After festival

  • Send survey within 48 hours
  • Edit and save all content to festival-specific folder
  • Update testimonial library
  • Write internal debrief: what worked, what didn't, metrics

Visual Style Guide (per content type)

Content Type Style Notes
Pain point reels Bold Red High energy, real footage, red overlays
Quote cards Sunset Vibes Warm gradients, cream text, illustrated characters
Product explainers Clean Studio White bg, phone mockups, line icons
Countdown graphics Bold Red Black/red, large numbers, urgency feel
Recap carousels Sunset Vibes Warm, celebratory, real photos with gradient overlay
Testimonials Raw/authentic No heavy styling — phone-quality video preferred

KPIs per Content Piece

Metric Target (per post)
Reel views 500+ (growing over time)
Reel saves 2%+ save rate
Story replies 3+ per story
Carousel shares 1%+ share rate
Link clicks (bio) 10+ per day during push
WhatsApp msg clicks 20%+ open rate
Sign-up conversion 10% of landing page hits

Created: 2026-03-21 Owner: Sales/BizDev Agent OKR: O1 — Prove WeDance can be a business (50 paid users across 3+ festivals at EUR 1/user)

Strategy

  • Target: 3+ festival partnerships, 50 paid users at EUR 1/user
  • Pitch: Free listing on WeDance + promotion via Social Dance TV (500K followers across Instagram, YouTube, Facebook)
  • Value prop for organizers: Interactive festival guide for attendees (schedule, partner matching, group dinners, ride sharing), gender balance analytics, workshop demand data — at zero risk to their existing ticketing
  • Value prop for dancers: Festival social layer for EUR 1 — find dance partners, join group dinners, share rides, coordinate activities
  • Pricing: First 10 dancers per festival FREE (seed community), EUR 1 per dancer after that
  • Channel: Kirill Korshikov (Social Dance TV, 500K followers) has direct organizer contacts across 36+ countries and attends ~50 festivals/year

Fit Assessment

Ideal Festival Profile

  • 100+ attendees (enough to seed 10 free + 40 paid)
  • Multi-day (2-4 days) — more coordination value
  • Social dance styles (salsa, bachata, kizomba, zouk, timba)
  • European location (timezone alignment, Kirill's circuit)
  • Organizer accessible (warm intro via Kirill or Amado preferred)
  • Shares schedule via PDF/Instagram (WeDance replaces this)

Top 3 Priorities (after Meneate pilot)

  1. Croatian Summer Salsa Festival — Large attendance, Kirill has direct contact, perfect timing (Jun/Jul)
  2. Yo Vengo de Cuba (Augsburg) — Close to Munich (Alex can attend), Amado has performed there, German market
  3. Salsa Open Berlin — Already in WeDance app data, 500+ attendees, major German city

Key intelligence from project files:

  • Kirill attends ~50 festivals/year across 36+ countries — he is the primary intro channel
